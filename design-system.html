[01] // PHILOSOPHY

The 6 Structural Laws

[LAW 01]

Zero Blurring & Zero Glow

Never use colorful drop-shadow glows (shadow-orange/30) or blur filters on buttons. Elevation is expressed via hard, solid black offset drop shadows (3px 3px 0px #000).

[LAW 02]

Precision 2px Structural Ink

All card outlines, inputs, and button boundaries are rendered with exactly border: 2px solid. Hairline borders are forbidden; lines represent real physical enclosures.

[LAW 03]

Micro-Fillet Corners (2px–4px)

No pill buttons or bubbly 16px cards. Radii emulate CNC-milled aluminum: rounded-[2px] for small tags, rounded-[3px] for buttons, and rounded-[4px] for master cards.

[LAW 04]

Physical Tactile Press

Buttons do not fade opacity on click. On :active, buttons physically translate down-right translate(2px, 2px) while shadow reduces to 0px, replicating an authentic mechanical push switch.

[LAW 05]

Bracketed Data Indexing

Headings and technical metrics are prefixed with bracketed numerals and uppercase tags: [01] // DIAL, [SYS_PASS], LOT 2026.

[LAW 06]

Drafting Crosshairs

Signature plus-signs (+) sit 6px inside card corners in muted zinc, anchoring the architectural blueprint aesthetic.
